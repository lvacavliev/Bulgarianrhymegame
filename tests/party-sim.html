Party mode transport test (BUILD.md 16.10, P1)

Simulates one host + phones in room A, and a host + 1 phone in room B. URL options: ?n=20 (phones), ?tap=10 (seconds of tapping), ?auto (start at once). Anonymous sign-ins are limited to 30 per hour per IP; the sessions are kept per slot, so re-runs reuse the same users.
